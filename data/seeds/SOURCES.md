# Initial candidate source

The 19 domains in `shopify-editorial.json` were transcribed from the outbound
website links in [Shopify's 4 July 2022 article](https://www.shopify.com/in/blog/look-good-perform-better-19-websites-built-on-shopify-that-are-a-visual-delight), inspected on
29 September 2026. Only candidate links are reproduced here, not article text.

This is a small development seed set, selected by Shopify's editors. It is
neither representative nor proof of present-day platform or business location.
Every domain must pass the live checks. Failed and blocked candidates remain
visible. No purchased domain list or search API was used.

Common Crawl adapters are available for source experiments; no Common Crawl
yield is claimed until a run is measured. Local imports must name their source.

## Follow-up leads

`shopify-editorial-followup.json` contains seven more outbound store links from
[Shopify India's clothing examples](https://www.shopify.com/in/blog/online-clothing-store),
[Shopify India's jewellery examples](https://www.shopify.com/in/blog/sell-jewellery),
and [Shopify's store examples](https://www.shopify.com/blog/shopify-stores).
The three articles were checked on 30 September 2026. These are candidates,
including a possible non-Shopify store, and have no accepted status until the
current storefront and Indian business evidence checks finish.
