THE RULES BEHIND THE RECORDS

Two separate checks.

01

Is the storefront on Shopify?

A Shopify shop identity must be supported by platform runtime assets and commerce markup. A Shopify image or footer credit on its own is insufficient.

02

Is the business based in India?

A business address must be supported by the merchant's own pages. Rupee prices, an Indian domain, and delivery to India do not settle this question.

03

Can you check the result?

Each field keeps its source page and observation date. Missing values stay missing. Unresolved stores are kept outside the verified export.

These are automated checks, not an independent audit. Review notes and exports preserve that distinction.
