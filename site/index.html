02 / DATA QUALITY

What the data contains

Missing values stay empty. Field coverage is measured only on the accepted stores.

Field completeness

WHAT “VERIFIED” MEANS

Two decisions, two kinds of evidence.

Shopify storefront signals and an Indian business address both had to be supported by merchant pages at collection time. Automated verification is not an independent accuracy audit.

03 / METHOD & SOURCES

How the list was built

Candidate directories supplied leads. The pipeline checked each merchant site before acceptance.

01

Find candidates

Collect publicly listed domains from Shopify editorial pages and store directories. Save source attribution for every candidate.

02

Check the storefront

Follow bounded, robots aware requests. Require corroborating Shopify identity, delivery, or commerce signals.

03

Check Indian business

Require address evidence on merchant pages. A .in domain, INR price, or shipping claim alone is insufficient.

04

Extract and review

Save contacts, socials, category, description, logo URL, state, missingness, and the evidence behind each field.

Candidate sources

A domain can appear in more than one source. Source totals are attribution counts, not unique store counts.
